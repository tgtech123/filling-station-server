## 1. What Is FuelDesk?

**FuelDesk** is an all-in-one management platform built specifically for filling stations. Think of it as the control room for your entire petrol station business — all from your phone or computer.

With FuelDesk you can:

- Track every litre of fuel sold, every shift worked, and every naira that comes in
- Manage all your staff with different levels of access based on their job role
- Record and monitor your tank levels, pump performance, and fuel prices
- Run a full gas department (CNG/LPG) including customer loyalty points
- Handle payroll, commissions, and expenses from one place
- Generate financial reports (income statements, cash reconciliation, P&L)
- Manage multiple branch stations from a single account
- Pay for your subscription and upgrade your plan through the platform
- Receive real-time alerts for low stock, completed shifts, and critical events

FuelDesk is web-based — you only need a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) and an internet connection. Nothing needs to be installed on your computer.

## 2. User Roles — Who Does What?

Every person who uses FuelDesk is given a **role**. Your role decides what you can see and what you can do. There are seven roles:

| Role | Who it is | What they can do |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Manager** | The station owner | Full access to everything — staff, finances, reports, settings, subscriptions |
| **Super Manager** | Owner of multiple branches | Same as Manager but across all branches at once |
| **Supervisor** | Senior operations lead | Approve shifts, manage attendants, view reports, submit dip readings |
| **Accountant** | Finance/accounting staff | View all financial data, run statements, manage payroll drafts, expenses |
| **Cashier** | POS operator | Record sales, manage daily reconciliation, handle lubricant transactions |
| **Attendant** | Pump operator | Start and end shifts, record pump readings, view own performance |
| **Admin** | FuelDesk platform team | Manage all stations, subscriptions, and platform settings (not your station staff) |

> **Important:** Only the Manager can create staff accounts, set roles, and delete records. If you are not sure what you can access, ask your station manager.

## 3. Getting Started — Station Registration

If you are a **new station owner**, follow these steps to register your station on FuelDesk.

### Step 1 — Go to the Registration Page

Open your browser and go to the FuelDesk website. Click **Get Started** or **Register** on the homepage.

### Step 2 — Choose a Plan

You will be shown the available subscription plans:

- **Free Plan** — Basic features, limited staff accounts. Good for trying the platform.
- **Pro Plan** — More staff slots, full reporting, PDF exports.
- **Pro Max Plan** — Everything in Pro plus advanced analytics.
- **Enterprise Plans** — For large or multi-branch operations.

If you are just starting out, select the **Free Plan**. You can upgrade later at any time.

> If you want to pay before registering, you can select a paid plan and complete payment through Paystack. Your plan will activate automatically once payment is confirmed.

### Step 3 — Fill in Your Personal Details (Step 1 of 4)

You will need to enter:
- **First Name** and **Last Name** — your name as the station owner
- **Email Address** — this is your login email (use one you check regularly)
- **Phone Number**
- **Address, City, and State**
- **Emergency Contact**
- **Profile Photo** (optional, but helpful for identification)

Click **Next** when done.

### Step 4 — Enter Station Details (Step 2 of 4)

Now fill in your station's information:
- **Station Name** — the official name of your station
- **Station Address, City, State, Country, Zip Code**
- **Station Email** (can be the same as your personal email or a business email)
- **Station Phone Number**
- **Owner Name** — the legal name of the owner (defaults to your full name)
- **License Number** — your DPR or relevant licence number
- **Tax Identification Number (TIN)**
- **Establishment Date** — when your station was founded
- **Station Logo/Photo** (optional)

Click **Next** when done.

### Step 5 — Operational Details (Step 3 of 4)

- **Business Type** — Independent station, Franchise, etc.
- **Number of Pumps** — how many fuel pumps you have
- **Operation Hours** — e.g., 24/7, 6am–10pm
- **Tank Capacity** — total storage capacity in litres
- **Average Monthly Revenue** — an estimate
- **Fuel Types Offered** — select all that apply (PMS, AGO, Diesel, Kerosene)
- **Additional Services** — e.g., Car wash, Auto shop, Convenience store

Click **Next** when done.

### Step 6 — Security & Password (Step 4 of 4)

- **Password** — choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters, mix letters and numbers)
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** — recommended; adds an extra security code at login
- **Notification Preferences** — choose which alerts you want to receive by email

Click **Register** or **Submit** to create your account.

### Step 7 — You Are In

After successful registration, you will be automatically logged in and taken to your **Manager Dashboard**. 

> Your subscription starts immediately. If you chose the Free Plan, you have a 30-day active period. Paid plans activate based on your billing cycle (monthly or yearly).

## 4. Logging In

Every staff member logs in using their assigned email and password.

### Step 1 — Open the Login Page

Go to the FuelDesk website and click **Login**.

### Step 2 — Enter Your Credentials

- **Email Address** — the email your manager used when creating your account
- **Password** — the password you were given (change it on first login — see Section 19)

Click **Login**.

### Step 3 — Two-Factor Authentication (if enabled)

If your account has 2FA turned on, you will receive a **one-time code** by email. Enter that code in the box shown and click **Verify**.

> The code expires in a few minutes. If it expires, click **Resend Code**.

### Step 4 — You Are Now Logged In

You will be taken to the dashboard that matches your role. Each role has a different home screen tailored to their responsibilities.

## 5. Forgot Your Password?

If you cannot remember your password:

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password**
2. Enter the **email address** linked to your account
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email from FuelDesk
4. Click the link in the email — it is only valid for **1 hour**
5. Enter your new password twice and click **Reset Password**
6. You can now log in with your new password

> If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam/junk folder.

**For station owners only:** The platform administrator can also trigger a password reset on your behalf from the admin panel. Contact FuelDesk support if you are completely locked out.

## 8. Shift Management

Shifts are the core of daily operations. Every time an attendant works at a pump, they open a shift, and when they finish, they close it.

### 8.1 For Attendants — Starting a Shift

1. Log in to your account
2. On your dashboard, click **Start Shift** (or go to **Shifts** in the menu)
3. Select the **pump** you are working on
4. Enter the **opening meter reading** — read the number displayed on the pump's totaliser and type it in exactly
5. Click **Start Shift**

Your shift is now active. The system has recorded your start time and opening reading.

### 8.2 For Attendants — Ending a Shift

1. Go to **Shifts** or click **End Shift** on your dashboard
2. Enter the **closing meter reading** — the current number on the pump
3. The system will automatically calculate:
   - **Litres sold** = Closing reading minus Opening reading
   - **Amount earned** = Litres sold × current fuel price
4. Click **End Shift** to submit

Your shift will now appear as **Pending Approval** in the supervisor's queue.

### 8.3 For Supervisors — Approving Shifts

1. Click **Shift Approval** in the left menu (or it appears on your dashboard)
2. You will see a list of shifts waiting for review
3. Click on a shift to see the full details (attendant name, pump, readings, litres, amount)
4. If everything is correct, click **Approve**
5. If there is an error, you can note the issue before approval

### 8.4 Viewing Shift History

1. Go to **Shifts** in the menu
2. You will see a table with all past shifts — date, attendant, pump, litres, amount, status
3. Use the search or date filter to narrow down results
4. Click any row to see the full shift detail

### 8.5 Scheduling Attendants

Supervisors and managers can create attendance schedules:
1. Go to **Schedule Shift** in the menu
2. Select the attendant and their shift type (Morning, Evening, Full-Time, etc.)
3. Set the date or recurrence
4. Click **Save Schedule**

## 9. Fuel Management — Pumps, Tanks & Pricing

### 9.1 Adding a Pump

> Done by the Manager or Supervisor

1. Go to **Fuel Management** > **Pumps** in the menu
2. Click **Add Pump**
3. Enter:
   - **Pump Name/Number** — e.g., "Pump 1" or "PMS Pump A"
   - **Fuel Type** — PMS, AGO, Diesel, Kerosene
   - **Nozzle Count** — how many nozzles this pump has
4. Click **Save**

### 9.2 Updating Pump Details

1. Go to **Fuel Management** > **Pumps**
2. Click **Edit** next to the pump you want to update
3. Make your changes and click **Save**

### 9.3 Scheduling Pump Maintenance

1. Go to **Fuel Management** > **Pumps**
2. Click the pump that needs maintenance
3. Click **Schedule Maintenance**
4. Enter the maintenance date and description
5. Click **Save**

The pump will be flagged for maintenance and will appear in alerts.

### 9.4 Managing Tanks

Tanks store your fuel. Keep their details up to date so your inventory is accurate.

**Adding a Tank:**
1. Go to **Fuel Management** > **Tanks** (or **Tank Status**)
2. Click **Add Tank**
3. Enter:
   - **Tank Name/Number**
   - **Fuel Type** stored in this tank
   - **Capacity** in litres
4. Click **Save**

**Submitting a Dip Reading:**
A dip reading is a manual check of how much fuel is physically in the tank. Supervisors submit these daily.

1. Go to **Dip Reading** in the menu
2. Select the tank
3. Enter today's measured level in litres (from the dip stick or gauging)
4. Click **Submit**

The system will compare this against expected levels based on recorded sales and flag any variances.

### 9.5 Updating Fuel Prices

> Done by the Manager

1. Go to **Fuel Management** > **Pricing** (or look for a **Prices** option in the menu)
2. Click **Edit Price** for the fuel type you want to update
3. Enter the new price per litre
4. Click **Save**

All future shift calculations will use the new price. Historical shifts keep their original prices.

### 9.6 Viewing Pump Performance

1. Go to **Pump Control** or **Pump Performance** in the menu
2. You will see each pump's:
   - Total litres dispensed today / this week / this month
   - Sales amount generated
   - Number of shifts served
   - Efficiency compared to other pumps

## 10. Lubricant Sales & Inventory

Lubricants (engine oil, gear oil, grease, etc.) are managed separately from fuel.

### 10.1 Adding a Lubricant Product

> Done by the Manager

1. Go to **Lubricant Management** in the menu
2. Click **Add Lubricant**
3. Enter:
   - **Product Name** — e.g., "Total Quartz 5W-30 1L"
   - **Barcode** — scan or type the product barcode
   - **Unit Price**
   - **Stock Quantity**
4. Click **Save**

### 10.2 Recording a Lubricant Sale

> Done by the Cashier or Manager

1. Go to **Lubricant Sales** in the menu
2. Click **New Sale** or **Add Transaction**
3. Scan the product barcode or search by name
4. Enter the quantity sold and the customer's payment method
5. Click **Record Sale**

The inventory count for that product automatically decreases.

### 10.3 Restocking Lubricants

When new lubricant stock arrives:
1. Go to **Lubricant Management** > **Purchases**
2. Click **Add Purchase**
3. Enter the product, quantity received, and cost

The inventory count increases by the quantity entered.

### 10.4 Viewing Lubricant Reports

Go to **Lubricant Sales** to view:
- **Daily Summary** — Sales for today
- **Weekly Summary** — This week's performance
- **Monthly Summary** — The full month at a glance

## 11. Gas Department (CNG / LPG)

The gas department is an optional module for stations that sell CNG or LPG. A manager must **enable** it before anyone can use it.

### 11.1 Enabling the Gas Department

> Done by the Manager

1. Go to **System Settings** or look for **Gas Settings** in the menu
2. Find the **Enable Gas Department** toggle
3. Turn it **ON**
4. The gas features will now appear in the menu for staff assigned to the gas department

### 11.2 Setting Up Cylinder Sizes

1. Go to **Gas Management** > **Cylinder Sizes**
2. Click **Add Cylinder Size**
3. Enter the size (e.g., 3kg, 6kg, 12.5kg) and price
4. Click **Save**
5. Use the toggle to enable or disable each size

### 11.3 Setting Gas Prices

1. Go to **Gas Management** > **Pricing**
2. Click **Set Price**
3. Enter the new price per kg or per cylinder
4. Click **Save**

### 11.4 Gas Staff Operations

**Starting a Gas Shift (Attendant):**
1. Go to **Gas Shifts**
2. Click **Start Gas Shift**
3. Select your pump or station assignment
4. Enter the opening reading if applicable
5. Click **Start**

**Recording a Gas Sale (Cashier):**
1. Go to **Gas Sales** > **Create Sale**
2. Select the customer (or create a new walk-in entry)
3. Select the cylinder size
4. Enter the quantity
5. Click **Create Sale**

The sale will appear as **Pending Confirmation**.

**Confirming and Dispensing a Sale (Attendant/Supervisor):**
1. Go to **Gas Sales** > **Pending Sales**
2. Click the sale to open it
3. Click **Confirm** and then **Dispense** once the gas has been given to the customer

### 11.5 Customer Orders (Home Delivery)

Customers can place orders for gas delivery:
1. The order appears in the **Cashier Inbox**
2. Cashier clicks **Confirm Order** to accept it
3. The order is assigned for fulfillment
4. Once delivered, the cashier marks it as complete

### 11.6 Gas Customer Management & Loyalty Programme

**Registering a New Gas Customer:**
1. Go to **Gas Customers**
2. Click **Add Customer**
3. Enter their **Name** and **Phone Number**
4. Click **Register**

**How the Loyalty Programme Works:**
- Every time a registered customer buys gas, they earn points based on the amount spent
- The Manager sets the **points per ₦1,000 spent** (e.g., 10 points per ₦1,000)
- When a customer has enough points (above the minimum threshold), they can redeem them for a discount
- The Manager sets the **naira value of each point** (e.g., ₦1 per point)

**Checking a Customer's Points:**
1. Go to **Gas Customers**
2. Search by phone number
3. Click the customer's name to see their points balance and transaction history

### 11.7 Gas Procurement (Ordering Gas Supply)

1. Go to **Gas Management** > **Procurement**
2. Click **Create Procurement**
3. Enter the quantity to order and select the supplier
4. Click **Submit**

The system emails the supplier automatically. When the gas arrives:
1. A Supervisor or Manager clicks **Confirm Delivery**
2. Then clicks **Validate** after checking quality and quantity

### 11.8 Gas Reconciliation

At the end of each day:
1. Go to **Gas Management** > **Reconciliation**
2. Review today's opening stock, sales, and remaining stock
3. Enter any discrepancies
4. Click **Submit Reconciliation** to close the day

## 12. Financial Reports & Cash Reconciliation

### 12.1 Daily Cash Reconciliation (Cashier)

At the end of the day, the cashier counts the physical cash and reconciles it with system sales.

1. Go to **Sales & Cash Report** or **Reconciliation**
2. The system shows the **expected cash** (total sales recorded)
3. Enter the **actual cash** counted
4. The system calculates the **variance** (difference)
5. Add a note if there is a variance (e.g., "Short ₦500 — customer paid short")
6. Click **Submit Reconciliation**

A copy of the reconciliation is saved and visible to the Manager and Accountant.

### 12.2 Dip Reading Submission (Supervisor)

See Section 9.4 above. After submitting a dip reading, the system compares tank levels against expected consumption from shift records. Any large variance triggers an alert.

### 12.3 Financial Reports (Accountant / Manager)

Go to **Reports** or **Financial Overview** to access:

**Income Statement:**
Shows all revenue earned and all expenses paid in a period. Tells you whether the station made a profit or loss.

**Profit & Loss Report:**
Similar to the income statement but broken down by product type (PMS, AGO, lubricants, gas).

**Cash Flow Report:**
Shows the movement of cash — money coming in from sales and going out for expenses.

**Balance Sheet:**
A snapshot of your assets (what you own) vs. liabilities (what you owe).

**Tax Report:**
Calculates tax based on revenue and applicable rates. Useful for filing.

**Sales Report:**
Detailed list of every sale — date, product, litres, price, amount, attendant.

**Shift Report:**
Summarises every shift — attendant, pump, hours, litres, revenue.

**Fuel Inventory Report:**
Stock movement — what came in (deliveries), what went out (sales), current stock.

### 12.4 Exporting Reports

Every report can be downloaded:
1. Open the report you want
2. Click **Export** (top right of the report)
3. Choose your format: **CSV** (spreadsheet-compatible), **Excel**, or **PDF**
4. The file will download to your device

### 12.5 Analytics & Trends

Go to **Analytics** to see:
- **Revenue trend** — daily, weekly, or monthly charts
- **Fuel breakdown** — which product sells the most
- **Staff performance** — who is selling the most
- **Period comparison** — this month vs. last month

## 13. Expense Management

Expenses are any costs your station incurs — fuel purchases, maintenance, salaries, utilities, etc.

### 13.1 Recording an Expense

> Done by Manager, Accountant, or Cashier (based on type)

1. Go to **Expenses** in the menu
2. Click **Add Expense**
3. Fill in:
   - **Category** — Product Purchase, Maintenance, Salaries, Utilities, Marketing, Other
   - **Description** — what the expense is for
   - **Amount**
   - **VAT Amount** — if there is an invoice with VAT, enter the tax amount
   - **Date**
   - **Reference / Invoice Number** (optional)
4. Click **Save**

The expense is recorded as **Pending**. The manager can review and approve it.

### 13.2 Approving Expenses

> Done by the Manager

1. Go to **Expenses**
2. Filter by **Status: Pending**
3. Review the expense details
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject**

Approved expenses are included in financial reports. Rejected ones are flagged for correction.

### 13.3 Expense Reports

Go to **Reports** > **Expense Report** to see a breakdown by category and time period. You can filter by date range and export to Excel or PDF.

## 14. Payroll & Commissions

### 14.1 Salary Management

Salary is processed monthly through an approval workflow:

**Step 1 — Accountant Creates a Draft:**
1. Go to **Payroll** or **Salary**
2. Click **Create Draft** for the current month
3. The system shows all active staff and their configured pay rates
4. Review and adjust individual amounts if needed
5. Click **Save Draft**

**Step 2 — Accountant Submits to Manager:**
1. Once the draft is reviewed, click **Submit Draft**
2. It moves to the manager's review queue

**Step 3 — Manager Approves:**
1. Go to **Payroll** > **Pending Drafts**
2. Review the salary batch
3. Click **Validate** to approve it

Once validated, the salaries are recorded and appear in the payroll history.

### 14.2 Commission Structures

Commissions reward staff for sales performance.

**Setting Up a Commission Structure:**
> Done by the Manager

1. Go to **Commissions** > **Structure**
2. Click **Add Commission Rule**
3. Choose the product type and the commission rate (percentage or fixed amount per litre/unit)
4. Set eligibility (which roles qualify)
5. Click **Save**

**Viewing Commission Payments:**
1. Go to **Commissions** > **Payment History**
2. See earned commissions by staff member and period

### 14.3 Consolidated Payroll (Multi-Branch)

If you manage multiple branches:
1. Go to **Consolidated Payroll**
2. Select all branches to include
3. View the combined payroll across all stations
4. Export for your records

## 16. Branches & Multi-Location Management

If your subscription plan supports multiple branches, you can manage several stations from one account.

### 16.1 Creating a Branch Station

> Done by the Manager (Super Manager level)

1. Go to **Branches** in the menu
2. Click **Add Branch**
3. Fill in the branch station's details (similar to the main station registration)
4. Click **Create Branch**

The branch is linked to your main station. Its financial data can be viewed separately or consolidated.

### 16.2 Inviting a Branch Manager

Each branch needs its own manager to handle day-to-day operations:

1. Go to **Branches**
2. Click on the branch you want to assign a manager to
3. Click **Invite Manager**
4. Enter the **email address** of the person you want to invite
5. Click **Send Invite**

The invited person receives an email with a link to set up their account. They will have Manager-level access only for that specific branch.

To cancel a pending invite:
1. Go to **Branches** > **Invitations**
2. Find the pending invite and click **Revoke**

### 16.3 Switching Between Stations

If you manage multiple stations:
1. In the top navigation, look for a **station selector** or your station name
2. Click it to see a dropdown of all stations you have access to
3. Click the station you want to switch to

Your dashboard will reload showing data for that station.

### 16.4 Consolidated Reports

1. Go to **Reports** or **Consolidated Payroll**
2. Choose **All Branches**
3. The report combines data from all your stations

## 17. Subscription & Billing

### 17.1 Viewing Your Current Plan

1. Go to **System Settings** (click the gear icon or find it in the menu)
2. Scroll down to the **Subscription & Billing** section

You will see:
- **Current Plan Name** (e.g., Free Plan, Pro Plan)
- **Price** per month or year
- **Started** — the date your current plan became active
- **Expires** — when your plan runs out
- **Days Remaining** (shown in red when 7 days or fewer remain)

### 17.2 Upgrading Your Plan

1. In **System Settings** > **Subscription & Billing**, click **Upgrade Plan**
2. A window appears showing available plans
3. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing (yearly saves you 16%)
4. Click the plan you want to upgrade to
5. Click **Proceed to Payment**
6. You are redirected to **Paystack** — enter your card details there
7. After successful payment, you are returned to FuelDesk
8. Your plan upgrades immediately and the new expiry date is set

> **Monthly plan:** Expires exactly 1 calendar month from today
> **Yearly plan:** Expires exactly 1 year from today

### 17.3 Billing History

In **System Settings** > **Subscription & Billing**, scroll past the plan card to see your **Billing History** — a table of every payment you have made, with date, amount, plan name, and billing cycle (Monthly/Annual).

### 17.4 Cancelling a Subscription

Cancellations are handled through FuelDesk support:
1. Click **Cancel Subscription** in the Subscription section
2. A window will appear with a **Contact Support** option
3. Send an email requesting cancellation — your plan stays active until the end of the current period

### 17.5 What Happens When a Plan Expires?

When your plan expires, your access is restricted:
- You can still log in and view existing data
- You cannot add new staff beyond the free tier limit
- Certain report exports may be disabled
- A banner on your dashboard will remind you to renew

To restore full access, simply upgrade your plan (Section 17.2).

## 22. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I am a new cashier. What do I need to do first?**
Your manager will create your account and share your login credentials. Log in, change your password immediately (Section 19.2), and then start recording daily sales and reconciliations.

**Q: An attendant started a shift but forgot to enter the correct meter reading. What now?**
A Supervisor or Manager can review the shift detail and flag it. Contact your supervisor — they can note the discrepancy before approving the shift.

**Q: How do I know when a tank is running low?**
You will receive a **Low Stock Alert** notification. Also check **Tank Status** in the menu — any tank below its threshold will be shown in amber or red.

**Q: Can I use FuelDesk on my phone?**
Yes. FuelDesk is a web app that works in any modern mobile browser. Open your browser, go to the FuelDesk website, and log in normally. For the best experience on mobile, use Chrome or Safari.

**Q: What happens if the internet goes down during a shift?**
Save your meter readings manually (write them down). Once the connection is restored, enter the readings into the system. Contact your supervisor to note the time gap.

**Q: My subscription expired but I cannot upgrade. What do I do?**
Go to **System Settings** > **Subscription & Billing** and click **Upgrade Plan**. If the payment fails, try a different card or contact your bank. You can also contact FuelDesk support for help.

**Q: I accidentally deleted a staff member. Can I recover them?**
Deleting a staff account removes their login access. Their historical data (shifts, sales) is preserved. To give them access again, create a new staff account for them. Contact support if you need to merge records.

**Q: Can two people be logged in as the same user at the same time?**
The system allows it, but it is not recommended. If you notice unfamiliar activity on your account, go to **System Settings** > **Active Sessions** and click **Logout Others** immediately, then change your password.

**Q: How do I add a new fuel type (e.g., Kerosene) after initial setup?**
Go to **Fuel Management** > **Pricing** and add the new product type, then add a tank and pump for it. Contact support if the product type is not listed as an option.

**Q: The billing history section shows empty. Why?**
Billing history only shows **successful** payments made through Paystack. If you are on the Free Plan, no payments have been made yet and the section will be empty.

**Q: What is the difference between a Dip Reading and a Shift Reading?**
A **Shift Reading** is recorded by an attendant at the pump (opening and closing meter values). It tracks litres sold per pump per shift. A **Dip Reading** is recorded by a supervisor with a physical measuring rod in the tank. It confirms the actual fuel level and catches any losses not recorded by the pump.
